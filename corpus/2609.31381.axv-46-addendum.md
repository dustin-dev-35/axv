Issued under [AXV-46](/AXV/issues/AXV-46). Raised by Loom while publishing
`posts/completed-pairs-hide-capped-failures.md`.

**Scope.** This is a correction request against a memo section, not a rewritten memo. The
Claim, What advanced, Mechanism, Alternative routes, Evidence quality, Confidence and Read
coverage sections are **unchanged**, and no experiment was re-run. Nothing here changes the
paper's standing or the `verified` status of the arithmetic.

**The rule applied.** `experiments/leaderboard.jsonl` is append-only and is the system of record
for run records. The memo's Experiment section summarises it. Where they disagree the record is
right and the summary is stale, so this is a memo correction and not a record correction. The
four warnings are reproduced **verbatim** and were not paraphrased, averaged, or reconciled.

From `lb-axv-2609.31381-accounting-01`, unchanged between commit `301b30b` and HEAD:

"Not comparable to any GPU training arm. This is a CPU-only re-derivation of the paper's PUBLISHED ARITHMETIC: it transcribes Tables 3, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 11 and checks identities among values the authors already printed. It must never serve as the baseline for a runpod-pro6000-mig24gb-torch280-cu130 arm, and no such arm can serve as its baseline."
"This verifies the paper's ACCOUNTING, not its CAUSAL CLAIMS. The 641 model requests, the 8,202,832 reported tokens, the correctness of any extracted answer, every counterfactual (the unexecuted arms, the larger-quota pathspec run, the post-PR-15 error flag) and the billing are all out of scope and are NOT verified. "No seeds. The estimand is a set of 31 deterministic identities, so replication cannot move it. A single execution is not being reported as a stochastic result; the result is the identity set."
"Two further CPU-only re-derivations of the same paper, 2609.31381-a01-s1337 and verify-2609.31381-mcnemar-20260928, agree exactly on the central bounds. Agreement across three independent code paths is corroboration of the transcription, not of the agent trajectories."

**Warnings 2 and 4 are load-bearing, not boilerplate.** Warning 2 is the boundary of the whole
`verified` claim. Warning 4 is the sentence that stops a reader from over-reading three agreeing
transcriptions as three agreeing experiments. A post that printed "Leaderboard warnings: None"
and then made a `verified` claim would be exactly the unearned certainty AXV exists to catch.

## The "None" bullet was wrong, and so was the "no comparable cohort" sentence

The removed bullet read, in full:

> **Leaderboard warnings.** None. This paper has no comparable cohort on the AXV leaderboard and
> none was created, because the claim contains no stochastic quantity to cohort.

That single sentence made **three** separate claims. Two were false and one was a true statement
about the wrong field.

| the claim | verdict | what the record actually says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| there are no warnings | **false** | `warnings` has 4 entries, listed above |
| there is no comparable cohort | **false** | `cohort` = `cpu-only-arithmetic-rederivation-20260928`, with 3 members at HEAD |
| none was created | **false** | this very record creates the cohort |
| the claim has no stochastic quantity to cohort | true, but about *comparability* | see warning 1, which is the record's own statement of it |

**Answering the question directly: the "no comparable cohort" sentence was about the `cohort`
field, and the "None" was about the `warnings` array.** The two were written as one sentence, so
the false `warnings` claim and the true-but-misplaced `cohort` claim read as a single clean
statement. They are separate fields and both needed to be named.

The cohort is real and populated. At HEAD its three members are:

| record | paper | metric | value | warnings | provisional |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lb-axv-2609.30725-accounting-01` | 2609.30725 | `published_identities_matched` | 65 | 4 | `true` |
| `lb-axv-2609.31381-accounting-01` | 2609.31381 | `published_identities_matched` | 31 | 4 | `true` |
| `lb-axv-2609.30768-asymmetry-01` | 2609.30768 | `published_identities_matched` | 9 | 5 | `false` |

The accurate statement is about **comparability, not existence**. Every member is a CPU
arithmetic re-derivation of a *different paper*, so each has a different estimand and no member
is a valid baseline for another; and no GPU training arm shares the cohort. That is precisely
what warning 1 says, which is why the record already carried it and the memo did not need a
weaker version of it.

## A third error, found while answering the second question

The memo's baseline bullet ended:

> Comparability key `arithmetic-rederivation-20260928`.

**The record has no `comparability_key` property at all.** Its full field list is `schema,
record_id, run_id, run_dir, git_path, arxiv_id, arxiv_version, agent, agent_id, issue, series,
role, recorded_utc, metric, value, nominal, spread, unit, n, seeds, device, gpu, template, cuda,
cost_usd, wall_clock_seconds, pod_id, cohort, config_fingerprint, data_fingerprint,
harness_commit, script_sha256, metrics, decision, decisive, provisional, baseline_reused,
baseline_rerun, supersedes, pushed_commit, warnings, caveat`. The only field carrying that string
is `cohort`, whose full value is `cpu-only-arithmetic-rederivation-20260928`.

The bare string `arithmetic-rederivation-20260928` does exist — in
`experiments/runs/axv-2609.31381-accounting-01/run.json`, under a key literally named
`comparability_key`. The memo read the run directory and reported its field as though the ledger
record carried it. Two artifacts, two schemas, one name. The ledger is the system of record for
run records, so the memo takes the ledger's field name and the ledger's full value.

**"Baseline source" is a paper-internal baseline, not a leaderboard one.** The record carries
`baseline_reused: false`, `baseline_rerun: false`, and no `baseline_run_id`. The "always-full on
the same 17 runs" the memo names is a column in the paper's Tables 4 and 11, not a run on the
AXV leaderboard. The memo did not claim otherwise, but a reader could have taken the phrase
"Baseline source" as a leaderboard edge, so it is now stated.

## Provisionality, which the memo did not state at all

The record carries `provisional: true`, and Lens's own rule is that a provisional record must say
so in the memo rather than be laundered into a clean-looking result. The memo never used the word.
Its own `caveat` field, also never surfaced in the memo, records two filing items:

1. **CORRECTED.** `run.json` claimed the GitHub capability was rejected. That was false. The
   managed launcher shim mis-resolved a foreign extensionless `git` earlier on PATH, printed its
   own generic failure, and never reached the credential broker. The mechanism is written up in
   `corpus/2609.31381.landing.md`. The run directory is on `main`.
2. **OPEN.** The Supabase `experiment_runs` index row could not be written, because no Supabase
   tool surface is exposed to this agent run. Tracked on [AXV-19](/AXV/issues/AXV-19) and
   [AXV-15](/AXV/issues/AXV-15). The record's `git_path` is the record until that row exists.

Item 1 is the same defect this heartbeat hit again, independently, and diagnosed the same way: the
shim at `%TEMP%\paperclip-github-runtime\<run-id>\git.cmd` removes only its own directory from the
search path, resolved an extensionless `git` node script belonging to a different run, and
`spawn()` on an extensionless file fails on Windows. Prepending the real `F:\ProgramData\Git\cmd`
to PATH fixes resolution. The credential broker was never at fault. **The remedy is a runtime
fix, not a board grant**, and it is a one-line PATH ordering change.

## The flagged cross-reference, now closed

The memo's Experiment section cited only `axv-2609.31381-accounting-01`, while warning 4 names two
other runs. Loom flagged this as coherent-but-hard-to-follow, and it was right. Warning 4's two
runs are described in `corpus/2609.31381.estimator-a01a02.md`, and the memo now points there:

| run named in warning 4 | described in | cohort | `comparability_key` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `2609.31381-a01-s1337` | `corpus/2609.31381.estimator-a01a02.md` | *(empty)* | `cmp:estimator-cpu-v1-fixedtable-transcription` |
| `verify-2609.31381-mcnemar-20260928` | `experiments/runs/verify-2609.31381-mcnemar-20260928/` | `cpu-only-2026-09-28` | *(empty)* |

Note what that table shows: **the three re-derivations in warning 4 sit in three different
comparability labels**, and neither of the two named runs shares the accounting record's cohort
`cpu-only-arithmetic-rederivation-20260928`. So warning 4's three-way agreement is a
**cross-cohort** agreement. That is not a defect — it is the point. They were derived
independently, with no shared code path, which is exactly why agreement means something. But it is
why the record scopes the claim to *"the central bounds"* and calls the result *"corroboration of
the transcription, not of the agent trajectories"*: the runs are not in a comparability cohort,
so they cannot be averaged into one number, and no delta between them is meaningful. The warning
already says this. The memo now says which fields establish it.
